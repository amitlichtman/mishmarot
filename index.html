How it works

Three steps, and the whole week is sorted

Set it up once, and every week after almost runs itself.

Set up the business

Shifts, hours, roles, and how many people each shift needs. Add your employees and they get sign-in details.

Employees submit

Each employee marks from their phone when they can, when they'd prefer not to, and when they can't, by the deadline you set.

Schedule and publish

Auto-assign by availability and roles, manual tweaks if needed, and one tap so everyone sees the schedule and gets notified.

Features

Everything you need to manage shifts

Built for real businesses: several roles, several managers, and people who come and go.

Submit from your phone

Employees submit availability in a few taps, and you see who has submitted and who hasn't.

Auto-assign

The system fills the schedule by availability, roles and priorities, and spreads shifts fairly.

Roles and permissions

Waiters, cooks, shift leads: each role sees only the shifts relevant to it. Owners, managers and employees.

Custom availability

"Available", "Prefer not", "Unavailable", or your own options, each with a color and a scheduling priority.

Submission deadline

Set a deadline, or lock submissions manually when you start scheduling.

Publish with a message

Publish the schedule in one tap, with a message to the team. Everyone sees their own shifts.

Hours reports

Hours per employee by week, month or any period, by schedule or by actual attendance, with Excel export.

An app on your phone

Install it on your home screen and it opens like an app. One account can work in several businesses.

Give away shifts

Can't make it? Offer the shift, suitable coworkers volunteer, and the manager approves in one tap.

Short-staffed shifts

A shift that needs people is marked right in the schedule, and suitable employees ask to join it.

Phone notifications

Get notified when the schedule is published, when there's a new request, and before the deadline. Everyone picks what they get.

Team directory

If the manager allows it: the team's contacts, with one-tap call and WhatsApp. Specific people can be hidden.

Swaps and requests

Someone can't make it? Sorted without phone calls

The employee offers their shift, and only employees who fit it see the offer. The manager gets notified, sees who volunteered, and approves in one tap. The schedule updates for everyone.

  • Only employees with the right role can ask
  • The manager is warned about missing rest or too many shifts
  • The manager decides whether giving away and requesting shifts is allowed

Attendance clock

A tablet at the door, and real hours at the end of the month

Any phone or tablet becomes an attendance clock in one tap. Employees pick their name, type their password, and clock in or out.

  • The time comes from the server, not the device clock

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no payment and no credit card needed to open a business and use every feature.

Do employees need to download an app?

No. Everything works in the browser, on any phone or computer. Anyone who wants can install the site on their home screen and it opens like an app.

How do employees get access?

The manager adds each employee with an email and password and sends them their sign-in details. Someone who already has an account in another business is added with that same account.

Can I manage several businesses or branches?

Yes. One account can be in several businesses, and you switch between them in one tap. Each business is managed separately, with its own team and settings.

What do I need for the attendance clock?

Any phone, tablet or computer with internet. A manager turns on clock mode, and employees punch with their own password.

Can employees swap or give away shifts?

Yes. An employee offers their shift, suitable coworkers volunteer, and the manager picks and approves. Employees can also ask to join short-staffed shifts. The manager can turn either option off.

Are there notifications?

Yes. Phone notifications when the schedule is published, when a request is answered, before the submission deadline, and for anyone who forgot to clock out. Everyone picks what they get, separately for each business. On iPhone, notifications work after installing the app on the home screen.

Where is the data stored?

On Google Firebase servers. Each employee sees only what they're allowed to: other employees' submissions and the full schedule are visible only to managers, and contact details only if the manager allows it. Privacy questions: privacy@dotheshift.com
